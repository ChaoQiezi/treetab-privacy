TreeTab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

TreeTab organizes your browser tabs in a tree in Chrome's side panel. It processes tab information on your device to provide this feature. It does not send your tab data to the developer or third parties, sell data, show advertisements, or use analytics.

Information TreeTab processes

  • Open tab titles, URLs, favicon addresses, tab IDs, window IDs, pinned and audio states, and tab group metadata.
  • Parent-child relationships between tabs, custom tab names, interface preferences, collapsed branches, and the last viewed release notes.
  • When you move a tab to the trash, its URL, title, favicon address, and closing time. The trash stores at most 50 records.
  • Recent undo information in temporary memory, temporary Zhouzhou companion animation state, and up to 200 local diagnostic error messages.

TreeTab does not request your name, email address, account credentials, payment or health information, precise location, or personal communications. It does not read the text, images, audio, video, forms, or other content of the web pages you visit. It does not record your clicks, pointer position, scrolling, or keystrokes, and it does not monitor network traffic.

How information is used

This information is used only to display and organize the tab tree, synchronize native Chrome tab groups, search open tabs, remember your preferences, recover closed tabs, restore recently closed branches, and diagnose local errors. TreeTab does not build an advertising profile or use the information for creditworthiness or lending.

Storage and retention

Tree relationships, custom names, preferences, trash records, and diagnostic logs are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device. Tab and group metadata is processed in extension memory while needed to show the current browser state. Undo data is kept in temporary memory. Zhouzhou's animation state is kept in session storage and cleared when the browser session ends or its window closes.

The trash retains at most 50 records. You can delete individual records, restore them, or clear the whole trash in the extension. Diagnostic logs retain at most 200 entries and can be cleared from the diagnostic-log panel. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension-managed local storage. Information that remains in Chrome's own open tabs or native tab groups is controlled by Chrome, not by TreeTab.

Sharing and external requests

TreeTab does not transmit your tab data to the developer or third parties and does not sell or share it. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking service. Its scripts, language files, release notes, fonts and graphical assets are included in the extension package; it does not execute remote code.

Restoring or opening a tab asks Chrome to visit that tab's URL in your browser. Displaying a website-provided favicon may ask Chrome to retrieve that icon. Those requests go to the relevant website, not to a TreeTab server. The feedback button opens a new tab with a mailto: address. No email is sent by the extension; if you choose to send a message in your email client, its contents and your email address are handled by your email provider under that provider's policies.

Permissions

  • tabs: display, search, organize, and recover tabs as requested by the user.
  • storage: preserve the tree, preferences, custom names, trash and diagnostic logs locally.
  • sidePanel: provide the extension's main interface in Chrome's side panel.
  • webNavigation: identify when a link in one tab opens another, so the tree can establish their parent-child relationship.
  • tabGroups: display and update Chrome's native tab groups.

Limited Use

TreeTab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new revision date. For privacy questions or support, contact the developer at chaoqiezi.one@gmail.com.

TreeTab 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日

概述

TreeTab 在 Chrome 侧边栏中以树状结构整理浏览器标签页。为实现此功能，它在你的设备上处理标签信息，不向开发者或第三方发送标签数据，不出售数据，不展示广告，也不使用分析统计。

TreeTab 处理的信息

  • 已打开标签页的标题、网址、网站图标地址、标签和窗口 ID、置顶与音频状态，以及标签组元数据。
  • 标签页的父子关系、自定义标签名称、界面偏好、分支折叠状态和最近阅读的更新说明。
  • 当你将标签页移入垃圾桶时，保存其网址、标题、网站图标地址和关闭时间。垃圾桶最多保存 50 条记录。
  • 临时内存中的最近撤销信息、粥粥动画的临时状态，以及最多 200 条本地诊断错误信息。

TreeTab 不要求你的姓名、邮箱、账号凭据、付款或健康信息、精确位置或个人通讯内容。它不读取所访问网页的文字、图片、音频、视频、表单或其他正文内容，不记录点击、鼠标位置、滚动或击键，也不监控网络流量。

信息用途

这些信息仅用于显示和整理标签树、同步 Chrome 原生标签组、搜索已打开标签、记住界面偏好、恢复已关闭标签、撤销最近关闭的分支，以及诊断本地错误。TreeTab 不建立广告画像，不将信息用于信用评估或贷款。

存储与保留

标签树关系、自定义名称、偏好、垃圾桶记录和诊断日志保存在你设备上的 Chrome 扩展存储中。当前标签和标签组元数据在扩展内存中按需处理。撤销数据只保留在临时内存中。粥粥动画状态保存在会话存储中，在浏览器会话结束或其所在窗口关闭时清除。

垃圾桶最多保留 50 条记录。你可以在扩展中逐条删除、恢复或清空垃圾桶。诊断日志最多保留 200 条，可在诊断日志面板中清空。卸载扩展会删除其扩展管理的本地存储。仍留在 Chrome 已打开标签页或原生标签组中的信息由 Chrome 控制，而非 TreeTab。

共享与外部请求

TreeTab 不向开发者或第三方传输、出售或共享你的标签数据，没有账号系统、广告、分析统计或跟踪服务。扩展的脚本、语言文件、更新说明、字体和图形素材均包含在安装包内，不执行远程代码。

恢复或打开标签页时，扩展会要求 Chrome 在你的浏览器中访问相应网址。显示网站提供的网站图标时，Chrome 可能向该网站请求图标。这些请求发往相关网站，而非 TreeTab 服务器。反馈按钮会打开一个带 mailto: 地址的新标签页，扩展本身不会发送邮件；如果你选择在邮件客户端中发送邮件，邮件内容和你的邮箱地址将由邮件服务商按其政策处理。

权限

  • tabs：按用户操作显示、搜索、整理和恢复标签页。
  • storage：在本地保存标签树、偏好、自定义名称、垃圾桶和诊断日志。
  • sidePanel：在 Chrome 侧边栏中提供扩展主界面。
  • webNavigation：识别某个标签页中的链接是否打开另一个标签页，以建立树中的父子关系。
  • tabGroups：显示和更新 Chrome 原生标签组。

有限使用

TreeTab 对通过 Google API 收到的信息的使用遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括有限使用要求。

政策更新与联系方式

如果本政策发生变化，更新版本将在本地址发布并注明新的修订日期。如有隐私问题或需要支持，请发送邮件至 chaoqiezi.one@gmail.com 联系开发者。
